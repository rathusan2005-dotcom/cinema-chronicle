### 📱 Sonic Chronicle v0.1.0 BETA — River of Sound, First Contact & Dossier Engine — *2026-10-29*

Bootstrapped the foundational intelligence engines for Rath's personal music autobiography (2020–2026). Standard year-end recaps flatten multi-year emotional growth into broad marketing summaries contaminated by 55k+ overnight sleep plays. This initial release establishes the mathematical models for fluid era transitions, concurrent life currents, and instant discovery velocity.

We designed the River of Sound as an organic Lee Byron silhouette streamgraph that flows symmetrically across 80+ months, revealing both macro life chapters and seasonal micro-waves (like autumn Laufey immersion loops). Concurrently, the First Contact engine captures lightning-strike moments when an artist hooks the user, tracking the journey from the initial gateway track into the full discography.

- ✨ **River of Sound Streamgraph**: Implemented Lee Byron organic streamgraph math with a live toggle between Monthly Flow and Quarterly Macro views, plus an Intentional Minutes vs. Play Count metric switcher.
- ✨ **Concurrent Currents & Crossover Detector**: Added real-time percentage balances for co-existing musical streams and automated alerts for milestone months when a new era dethroned the previous #1.
- ✨ **Artist Lifecycle Dossier**: Built an interactive deep-dive modal detailing the Gateway Track (*ZAZA* for JanZee), All-Time Crown Track (*Bad Love*), Peak Share of Ear (41.2% in Oct 2024), Catalog Friction, and 30-Day Binge Acceleration Trajectory.
- ✨ **Circadian Mood Matrix**: 7×24 day-hour density matrix highlighting Late Night introspective acoustics vs Golden Hour scores vs Midday drill surges.
- ⚙️ **Smart Cleanser Architecture**: Foundation rules isolating overnight sleep playlists (`🥱chilling and sleeping ?!?`, `kan mooditu thoongu da`) and sub-10s accidental skips.
